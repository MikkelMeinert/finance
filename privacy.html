Privacy

finance, a personal budgeting app. Last updated 18 November 2026.

What this is

finance is a private tool that one person, its owner, uses to follow their own household budget. It is not offered to anyone else. There are no user accounts and no sign-up.

What data it handles

Balances and transactions from the owner's own bank accounts. They are fetched read-only through Enable Banking, after the owner has approved access at the bank. The app cannot move money.

Where the data is kept

The data is encrypted before it is stored in a private repository. It is decrypted only on the owner's own devices. It is not shared with, sold to or processed for anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

This website

These pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. They set no cookies and contain no analytics or trackers. The numbers shown when the app is opened without the owner's key are made-up examples. GitHub, as the host, may log technical details of visits such as IP addresses.

Withdrawing access

The owner can withdraw the bank consent at any time, at the bank or at Enable Banking. Consent also expires on its own and must be renewed by the owner.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the owner of the GitHub account that hosts this page.
